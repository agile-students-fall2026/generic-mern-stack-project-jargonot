# Contributing to Jargonot

Thanks for contributing to Jargonot! Contributions should support Jargonot's goal of helping students explore research and understand papers outside their field.

## Team norms
Please collaborate in line with our team norms!

### Team values
Our team mainly communicates through Discord. Members who needs help should immediately seek for help from the rest of the team. If conflicts or disagreements are found, the issues will be addressed and discussed in the next standup meeting. 

Team members are expected to respond within 6 hours upon receiving messages directed at them.

### Sprint cadence
Each of our sprint takes two weeks.

### Daily standups
Daily standups occur every Monday and Wednesday at 9:00 AM, and Friday at 12:00 PM. Members are expected to be present synchronously, and members will not cover for other members who do not participate. A member who makes no progress on a task for two standups or more in a row will be reported to management.

## Git workflow
Jargonot follows the Git feature-branch workflow. To make changes, contributors should:
1. Pull from the master branch
2. Create a local branch with a name that identifies its task
3. Make and commit changes locally. A feature branch should live no more than 3 days to prevent merge hells.
4. Merge with the latest trunk branch and push branch to remote repository.
5. Issue a pull request and wait for a peer review.

## What to contribute
Jargonot welcomes contributions that help students and researchers discover relevant papers and understand research outside their field. 

To get started, check the project task board for available `tasks` or `spikes` and choose work that fits your experience. If you find a bug or have an improvement in mind that is not already tracked, share it with the team first so you can agree on the scope and avoid duplicating work. For larger or user-visible changes, discuss the proposal with the team before implementation.

Keep contributions focused, explain the problem they solve and include relevant tests or documentation updates. In your pull request, please summarize the change and how you implemented it.
